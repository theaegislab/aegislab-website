## Our Mission

We work to advance early detection and treatment of cancer. We are coders and quantitative
scientists who build new algorithms for analyzing biological data, specializing in DNA mutations.

New technology to measure biology, together with recent advances in AI, will transform our
understanding of cancer origins and our ability to treat it. We develop and evaluate our
tools in collaboration with clinicians.

## Core Values

Intellectual pursuit is one of the great perks of being a scientist — something to be savored
by anyone so inclined. We want to train new scientists and open this way of life to anyone
with the curiosity and dedication to pursue it. Respect, collegiality, and inclusivity shape
our creative environment.

## Environment

The group is part of the Pathology Department at Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center (BIDMC)
and Harvard Medical School, in Boston, Massachusetts.
